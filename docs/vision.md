# Vision

## Name

**Mentalmatics** — a blend of "mental math" and "mathematics",
reflecting the app's focus: training mathematical calculation
entirely in your head.

## Product Idea

A cross-platform app that lets adult users train and improve their
mental math at their own pace — with no accounts, no cloud
requirement, and no ads.

## Target Audience

Adults who want to improve their mental math skills through
self-directed training. No focus on schools/classrooms, no
teacher-student structure.

## Core Value (USP)

Three exercise areas whose task types are granular and freely
combinable (e.g. addition only, subtraction only, any mix — no rigid
fixed modes):

1. **General mental math** — basic arithmetic operations (addition,
   subtraction, multiplication, division) up to advanced operations,
   all difficulty levels selectable.
2. **Real-life everyday scenarios** — e.g. unit-price comparison at
   the supermarket ("Which package is cheaper per 100 g?"), discount
   calculation, tipping.
3. **Computing-related math** — conversion between positional numeral
   systems. Quick selection for binary/octal/hexadecimal, plus a
   freely configurable custom base, with free choice of source and
   target system (e.g. binary → decimal, custom base → hexadecimal).

Additionally, a **"Tips & Tricks" section**: purely informative texts/
examples on calculation techniques (e.g. the Trachtenberg system) and
technical explanations (e.g. how to convert between number systems) —
initially with a small amount of content, part of the MVP.

Focus on your own pace and your own control (timer on/off, progress
tracking on/off).

## Non-Goals (explicitly outside the vision)

- No competitive/leaderboard feature
- No multiplayer functionality
- No user accounts or cloud sync (v1)
- No ads, no telemetry/analytics tracking
- No adaptive AI-driven difficulty adjustment (v1) — deliberate,
  manual difficulty selection is the priority
- Tips & Tricks section without interactive quizzing (v1) — purely
  informative

## Design Guardrails

- **Privacy-first**: all data stays local on the device.
- **Platform openness**: full support for Android (Play Store),
  GrapheneOS (F-Droid, Obtainium), and iOS (iPhone, iPad; App Store) —
  without forcing proprietary Google Play Services.
- **Free**: no paywalls, no ads. A voluntary donation option (e.g. Buy
  Me a Coffee) is not ruled out, but not yet finally decided.
- **Multilingual from the start**: MVP in German and English, with
  further languages planned.
- **Accessibility**: not an MVP blocker, but considered in design
  decisions from the start, so it doesn't need to be retrofitted at
  significant effort later.

## Vision of Success

Users open the app regularly because practicing is engaging,
self-paced, and pressure-free — and they notice in everyday life
(e.g. while shopping) that their mental math has visibly improved.
